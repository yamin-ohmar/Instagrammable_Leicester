# Instagrammable Leicester Website Walkthrough

Follow these steps to work your way through the website:

1.  **Sign Up** (for new users)

- Go to the **Instagrammable Leicester** website page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate "**Login**" button and click on it. You will be redirected to the **Login or Signup** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app/pages/login
- Locate **Don't have an account? Sign up now:** section and enter your new user name and password.
- Click on "**Sign Up**" button.
- If your sign up is successful, you will be redirected to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app

---

2. **Sign Out** (after Sign Up or Login)

- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate your **user name** on the page and click on it. You will be redirected to your **Profile** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app/pages/profile
- Locate "**Sign Out**" button and click on it. "*Are you sure you want to sign out?*" message will prompt.
- If you click **Cancel** button, you will continue to stay on the **Profile** page.
- If you click **OK** button and your sign out is successful, you will be redirected to the **Home** page.

---

3.  **Login** (for returning users)

- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate "**Login**" button and click on it. You will be redirected to the **Login or Signup** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app/pages/login
- Locate **Login to your account:** section and enter your user name and password.
- Click on "**Login**" button.
- If your **user name** is not right, "*Username does not exist. Please sign up first.*" message will prompt.
- If your **password** is not right, "*Incorrect password. Please try again. You have left 2 attempts*" message will prompt.
- If your login is successful, you will be redirected to the **Home** page.

---

4.  **Font Size Adjustment** (for accessibly)

- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate "**A**" (bigger size) button and click on it. You will notice that the font size of the website content becomes bigger.
- Locate "**A**" (smaller size) button and click on it. You will notice that the font size of the website content becomes smaller.

---

5.  **Link to Instagram Posts** (via image display)

- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate any **image** from the list of locations and click on it. You will be redirected to the related **Instagram post** in a new tab/window.

---

6.  **Viewing Reviews** (before logged in)
(*this procedure needs you to be logged out of the website first*)

- If you are logged in, follow section 2. **Sign Out** to sign out.
- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- You will notice that you see the **Review(s)** section under any location from the list but you do not see the reviews. It asks you to "*Please login to write about your experience or read other visitors' experiences...*".
- Locate any "**Login for Reviews**" button and click on it. You will be redirected to the **Login or Signup** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app/pages/login
- Follow section 3. **Login** to login.

---

7. **Posting Reviews** (after logged in) 

- Go to the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app
- Locate **Review**" input box with the text "*Please leave a review about this place...*" under the **Review(s)** section and click in it. You will notice that "**Submit Reivew**" button is disabled.
- Start typing into the box, you will notice that "**Submit Reivew**" button becomes enabled. And you will also notice the **character count** at the bottom right corner of the box is counting up.
- If you type in more than *250 characters*, you will notice that the **character count** becomes red and "**Submit Reivew**" button becomes disabled.
- After typing in within *250 characters*, click on "**Submit Reivew**" button. The **Home** page will be re-loaded and you will find your review posted under the **Review(s)** section. You will also notice the **review(s) count** went up.

---

8. **Viewing Reviews** (after logged in)
(*this procedure needs you to be logged in with a different account*)

- To view the reviews from other accounts, you will need to sign in with a different account.
- If you are logged in, follow section 2. **Sign Out** to sign out.
- Then, follow section 1. **Sign Up** to create a different account.
- At the **Home** page at URL: https://co2104-cw2-yo53.netlify.app, you will find your previous account's review(s).
- Follow section 7. **Posting Reviews** to see reviews being posted from different accounts.
