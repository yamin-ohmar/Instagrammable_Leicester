Instagrammable Locations in Leicester

Here are 5 amazing places in Leicester for you to explore!

  1. Castle Gardens

    (Visited date: 23/03/2024 - Spring)

    Leicester Castle Gardens is a historic site located in Leicester, England. It's a beautiful green space situated in the heart of the city, adjacent to Leicester Castle. The gardens offer a peaceful retreat for locals and visitors alike, with its picturesque surroundings and historical significance. It's a lovely place to explore and relax, offering a glimpse into Leicester's rich heritage.

    • Town Hall Square

      (Visited date: 20/01/2024 - Winter)

      Leicester Town Hall Square features a fountain that is a prominent landmark in the city center. The fountain is located in front of the Leicester Town Hall, which is an impressive Victorian building. The fountain itself is an ornate structure, often adorned with colorful lights during special events or festivals. It serves as a focal point for gatherings and events in the square, and it's also a popular spot for locals and tourists to relax and enjoy the surroundings.

      • Abbey Park

        (Visited date: 04/09/2023 - Autumn)

        Abbey Park is a picturesque public park located in Leicester, England. It covers a significant area along the River Soar and is a popular destination for both locals and visitors. The park features beautiful gardens, open spaces, and tree-lined pathways, providing a tranquil escape from the city's hustle and bustle.

        One of the notable features of Abbey Park is the remains of the 12th-century Leicester Abbey, which can still be seen within the park. The ruins add a sense of history and character to the surroundings. Additionally, the park offers amenities such as playgrounds, sports facilities, and a boating lake, making it a versatile recreational space for people of all ages.

        • New Walk

          (Visited date: 25/10/2023 - Autumn)

          Leicester's New Walk is a charming pedestrian promenade in the city, known for its picturesque tree-lined avenue. It's one of the finest examples of a Georgian pedestrian street in the UK and was constructed in the early 19th century. The New Walk runs for about half a mile from Leicester's Victorian railway station to De Montfort Square.

          The walk is also home to a variety of shops, cafes, and businesses, making it a vibrant hub of activity in the city. The New Walk Museum and Art Gallery is located on New Walk in Leicester. It's one of the city's major cultural institutions and a popular destination for visitors interested in history, art, and natural sciences.

          • Castle View

            (Visited date: 14/06/2023 - Summer)

            Castle View is a street located near St. Mary de Castro Church in Leicester. It's named for its proximity to Leicester Castle, which historically stood nearby. The area around Castle View and St. Mary de Castro Church has a rich history dating back to medieval times. Visitors to Castle View can appreciate the medieval charm of the area and enjoy glimpses of Leicester's rich history as they explore the surroundings.
